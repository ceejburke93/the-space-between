**Decision.** Enter Gaming. Target Best First Five Minutes. New Experience fits if the team confirms the project was conceived and built within the competition window. ## What a judge sees

The first Leaf capture contains two huts, two creatures, trees, a bright crossing, a prompt, and a Menu button. At film size I could locate Leaf but had to look twice to see its foot testing the hand. The foreground relationship is the strongest part of the game and the least legible part of this frame. Record the opening from a closer, stationary viewpoint. Keep the hand, Leaf’s foot, and the edge of its safe shore in the same shot. Keep the wider view for the crossing and the landscape reveal. Verify the shot at ordinary laptop video size with sound muted.

## Findings in order

1. **The first invitation can contradict the creature’s body.** HandMeeting accepts either hand. Before weaving, the idle yaw in src/between.ts selects hand zero, including when the offered hand belongs to the other side. The foot can reach the correct place while the body points somewhere else. Aim at the offered hand while Leaf tests it, then lift its gaze to the player. Check left-only and right-only input.
2. **A tentative player can lose the opening.** Withdrawing before trust sends HandMeeting through settling to stage five. Reoffering cannot restart the meeting until the encounter resets. The stage-four prompt can even tell this player that Leaf knows them. Distinguish interrupted contact from earned trust. Return Leaf to an inviting pose after interruption. Let a player reoffer a hand or continue across the bridge by choice.
3. **The latest hands-only evidence starts from a practiced configuration.** The runner enables assistance and reduced reach during preparation. A new save starts with those settings and sound off. The latest strict runs cover menus and the three Leaf encounters. Historical records cover all twelve using hand gestures, with controller navigation in older runs. Use an isolated clean browser profile for the first-visit audit, preserve existing saves, and observe a novice before providing hints. Separately validate all twelve and the finale using strict hands-only navigation.
4. **The strongest payoff is easy to miss.** The garden and finale already remember player choices. For example, the perch choice changes the timing of the companions’ crossing. If the film promises kindness passed from one creature to another, use the actual branch and show both creatures in one continuous shot. Until that shot reads on its own, lead the pitch with the palm encounter.
5. **Submission work remains.** The local hosting check proves the build resolves under a project path. The repository records a local workflow and deferred publishing. The film is a shot plan. Confirm membership, division eligibility, a working judge-accessible URL, a public video under three minutes, and entry copy with inspiration, implementation, future plans, and target launch date. The deadline is November 18 at noon Pacific. [Submission rules](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules)

## Four-element implementation and voice pass, November 4

Leaf now looks toward the hand that is testing its palm. Withdrawing before trust returns the invitation to its open state, and the player can offer either hand again. During the optional first tests across Water, Fire and Earth, the creature attends to the tracked hand. Water rises, Fire warms as its circle forms, and Earth leans into the rising stones. These are local reactions around the creature, not camera motion.

The text review found two mismatches with one-hand play. Water and Earth now say “one hand” where the gestures accept one. When tracking is absent, the prompt distinguishes Leaf’s default two-hand bridge from the one-hand chapters and names controllers when that is the selected input. The phrase “You’re doing beautifully” still assigns emotion to the player. Consider removing it in the next writing pass. The game can show the traveler’s trust without telling the player how to feel.

Audio is original procedural Web Audio, with no external clips. The mix has separate music, effects, ambience and UI paths, four filtered habitat beds, located listeners and emitters, short creature cues, vegetation rustles, adaptive chapter music, focus muting and a limiter. No sound is needed to solve a puzzle. The existing mute check passes. As a critical listening review, the main exposure is that longer ambience beds use the same looping noise source with different filters. On headphones, check repetition, stereo orientation when turning, cue level over narration, and transitions as chapters change. One timed emulator audio check previously passed for enabled sound and full mute. This does not establish a human listening assessment.

The four hands-only opening replay reached each chapter’s first encounter and returned a creature home. The hand-off report is saved under artifacts/emulator/ with the November 4 invitations run. Desktop emulation checks input and saved state. It cannot establish how a first-time player interprets the creature’s intent. The novice observation remains important.

## A separate award path

Play4Change’s 2027 Original game for social change is a better additional award target than the paid NewImages XR Festival competition. The current Play4Change call is free, accepts English entries, permits one project in multiple award categories, and closes December 31, 2026. This game may fit through empathy and care, but theme alone is insufficient. Its rules exclude entries that do not make an explicit, reasoned connection to social change. Frame the entry around practicing patience, care, and shared responsibility, then provide a small observed playtest result that shows what participants noticed or carried beyond the session. Do not claim measurable social impact from the game’s theme alone. [Official Play4Change call](https://www.play4change.it/en/play4change-en/)

NewImages Festival’s paid XR Competition also accepts narrative VR games completed in 2026 or 2027, with its final call closing December 22, 2026. This is a secondary option if the team wants an exhibition submission and can justify the €60 individual-talent fee. It is an XR festival competition, not a dedicated game award. [Official XR Competition](https://www.newimages.art/calls-for-projects)
